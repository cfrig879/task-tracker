About us

We build calm tools that help you plan honestly and learn from how time actually goes.

Our mission

Cadence exists to help people build healthier routines through reflection. We focus on the gap between what you planned and what happened — not as judgement, but as information you can use.

What we do

Cadence is a lightweight task and time tracker that compares estimates to actual time, then surfaces small behavioral insights.

It’s a reflection system — not a productivity whip.

Why we built it

Most tools celebrate completion. We wanted something that supports awareness: how long things really take, what pulls you off course, and what patterns repeat.

What we believe

  • Calm design beats noisy dashboards.
  • Small friction compounds into abandonment.
  • Insight changes behavior more reliably than guilt.
  • Your time data should stay private by default.

What’s next

  • Demo mode improvements
  • Better CSV import validation
  • Mobile polish
  • Pagination and filtering
