Who is Cadence?

Cadence is your planning companion — helping you understand your time, not just track your tasks.

Cadence helps you plan with reality in mind

Cadence looks at the space between what you expected and what actually happened.

By turning everyday tasks into useful reflection, Cadence helps you notice patterns, adjust your plans, and make your next day feel a little more realistic.

What Cadence does

Cadence tracks tasks, due dates, categories, estimated time, and actual time spent.

Over time, those details help reveal where your estimates are accurate, where time tends to slip, and which tasks may need more attention.

Think of Cadence as a mirror for your planning habits.

Why we built it

Most productivity tools focus on checking things off a list. But finishing tasks doesn’t always mean you’re using your time well.

Cadence helps you reflect, not just complete — so you can improve how you plan, not just how you perform.

What we believe

  • You can’t improve what you don’t understand.
  • Small insights lead to meaningful change.
  • Planning should reduce stress, not create it.
  • You should always feel in control of your data.

Why Cadence exists

Cadence started from a simple problem: underestimating how long things would take.

Days would begin with a plan, but end with tasks taking longer than expected, or not getting done at all. Over time, it became clear that the issue wasn’t effort — it was visibility.

Without a clear picture of how time is actually spent, it’s easy to overcommit, misjudge effort, and repeat the same patterns.

Cadence helps turn guesswork into understanding — so your plans get better over time.

Privacy

Cadence is designed to collect only what it needs to function. Your tasks and time data are not shared or sold.

If analytics are added in the future, they will be transparent and optional.
